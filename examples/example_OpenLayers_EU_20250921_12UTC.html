Surface Weather Observations for Europe, 21 September 2025, 12 UTC
This is an OpenLayers example page showcasing station-model-symbology, a JavaScript module for dynamically generated, vector-based station model symbols from encoded SYNOP data, depicting weather observations on the client-side, for web maps.
Data source: National meteorological services through Ogimet

Leaflet demos: EU / 21 Sep 2025, HU / 6 Sep 2025, HU / 17 Dec 2025
Other OpenLayers demos: HU / 6 Sep 2025, HU / 17 Dec 2025

Project page: https://github.com/balladaniel/station-model-symbology
